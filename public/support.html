Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a receipt?

Tap the "Scan" tab at the bottom of the app, then tap "Upload Image". Choose a photo from your camera roll or take a new photo. Our AI will automatically extract the vendor, amount, date and category.

How does the free trial work?

New users get a 3-month free trial with full access to all features. No credit card is required to start. You'll be prompted to subscribe before your trial ends.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the App Store on your iPhone, tap your profile picture, then tap Subscriptions. Find TaxTracker and tap Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.

Can I export my data?

Yes! Go to the Expenses page and tap "CSV" or "JSON" to download your expense data. On the Tax Reports page you can also download a PDF summary for your accountant.

How does vehicle mileage tracking work?

Go to the "Vehicle" tab, tap "Track Live Trip" to use GPS tracking, or tap "Add Trip" to enter odometer readings manually. Trips are automatically calculated and categorised for tax purposes.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your financial information is private and is never shared or sold to third parties.

What is the Deduction Diary?

The Deduction Diary lets you record recurring tax-deductible items such as phone bills, internet, and home office costs — anything not covered by a single receipt.
