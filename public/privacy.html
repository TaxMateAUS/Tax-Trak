TaxTracker Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 May 2026

Effective date: 11 May 2026

Overview

TaxTracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you use the TaxTracker mobile application and website.

By using TaxTracker, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: your email address and name when you register
  • Expense data: receipts, amounts, dates, vendor names, categories and notes you enter
  • Vehicle and trip data: vehicle details, trip locations, odometer readings and purposes
  • Diary entries: deduction records and notes you create
  • Receipt images: photos you upload for scanning

Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device type, operating system version and app version
  • Usage data: features used and general app interaction patterns
  • Location data: GPS coordinates during live trip tracking (only when you explicitly start a trip — this can be disabled in iOS Settings)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate and maintain the TaxTracker app
  • Process and extract data from receipt images using AI
  • Calculate mileage and generate tax reports
  • Manage your subscription and process payments via Stripe
  • Send account-related emails (e.g. subscription confirmations)
  • Improve and debug the app

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on encrypted servers. All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and financial information.

Receipt images and financial data are stored only for as long as your account is active or as required to provide the service.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy
  • AI processing services — receipt images are sent to an AI service solely to extract expense data. Images are not stored or used for training.
  • Law enforcement — where required by applicable law or legal process

Location Data

TaxTracker requests access to your device's location only when you use the Live Trip Tracker feature. Location data is used solely to calculate trip distance and record start/end addresses. You can disable location access at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → TaxTracker.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your data (via the CSV/JSON export feature in the app)

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@taxtracker.app.

Children's Privacy

TaxTracker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the app or by email. Your continued use of TaxTracker after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
